# WaveLabs Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

**Vision.** Make onchain activity feel like a signal you can read at a glance - warm, analog and honest about what it does not know.

**Mission.** Build a small, dependable desk for Solana: live market context, a home for the WaveLabs token, and tools that stay calm when the data gets noisy.

## Principles

1. **Say only what is stated.** Every external fact - an address, a link, a data source - is a single record. If a record is not stated, the interface shows it as inert rather than guessing.
2. **Fail softly.** If a feed drops, keep the last good reading and mark the gap.
3. **Respect attention.** Motion explains change; it never decorates for its own sake, and it switches off on request.

## Roadmap

**Stage 01 - Carrier.** Brand, site and a live market board. The pinned row waits behind a placeholder.

**Stage 02 - Broadcast.** The contract address is published. The pinned row activates and pulls price, volume and liquidity from public sources.

**Stage 03 - Amplify.** Watchlists, per-token detail views and alert hooks.

**Stage 04 - Resonance.** Community tooling and shared dashboards shaped by the people who use the desk.
